Logo Usage Guidelines

Primary Logo Usage

The color logo is the preferred version for digital platforms, portfolios, social media, and creative branding.

Black & White Version

Used when color printing is unavailable or for minimal branding requirements.

Reverse Version

Used on dark backgrounds to maintain readability and brand consistency.

Favicon Usage

A simplified version of the logo designed for browser tabs and small icons.

Configuration Rules
  • Do not stretch or distort the logo.
  • Maintain clear spacing around the logo.
  • Use the reverse logo only on dark backgrounds.
  • Use the favicon for small digital applications.
